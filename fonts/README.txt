Drop your Times New Roman .ttf files here.
Rename them to match what style.css expects:

  TimesNewRoman.ttf         ← regular  (required)
  TimesNewRoman-Bold.ttf    ← bold     (optional)
  TimesNewRoman-Italic.ttf  ← italic   (optional)

Windows font folder filenames → rename to above:
  times.ttf   →  TimesNewRoman.ttf
  timesbd.ttf →  TimesNewRoman-Bold.ttf
  timesi.ttf  →  TimesNewRoman-Italic.ttf

On Mac: open Font Book → right-click "Times New Roman" →
"Show in Finder". The files are .ttc (font collection).
Convert to individual .ttf at transfonter.org (free).
